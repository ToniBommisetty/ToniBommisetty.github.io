# Desi City

An open-world 3D game set in real Indian towns, made to play right in the browser on a phone. Walk, drive, fight, and fly through streets built from real map data. Visit temples, take on hero missions, and play online with friends, all without installing anything.

**Play now:** https://tonibommisetty.github.io/desi-city/

Works best in Safari or Chrome on a phone in landscape. It runs on desktop too.

---

## Highlights

### A real India to explore
- Real streets, buildings, parks and landmarks for Indian towns are streamed from OpenStreetMap, starting in **Rayachoty, Andhra Pradesh**. Tirumala is also included.
- An India map with every state. Pick a state and city and the world loads around you.
- Day and night cycle with a retro neon look: warm sunsets, glowing signs and wet-looking roads.
- The town is busy with people walking, traffic, auto-rickshaws, police and ambulances, and crows. Cattle stay in fields and parks, off the roads.

### Your hero
- A custom 3D hero with a sculpted face, quiff hair, a tropical vest and a gold chain.
- Walk, run, drive, cycle, climb, and do a namaste.

### Temples
- Walk inside temples and see the deity, with a pujari performing the rituals.
- Visiting a temple gives you a moment of peace and restores health.

### Places to go
- Enter houses and buildings through visible doors. Take the **stairs or the lift to the roof**.
- Tea stalls, restaurants, hospitals, banks, malls, cinemas and gyms all have something to do.
- **Outdoor gyms** on beaches and in parks: pull-ups, bench press and curls make your hero stronger.
- **Park cycles** to ride around town.
- **Movie shoots** appear in random spots. Walk on set and join the scene.

### Map and navigation
- A full city map you can pinch to zoom, with icons for every shop, temple, bank and more.
- **📋 Find places:** search by name or pick a category (Food, Tea, Temple, Hospital, Bank, Mall, Gym, Powers and more). Places are sorted by distance and direction. Tap **🧭 Go** to get a route.
- A blue route path appears only when you choose a destination.

### Fighting
- **HIT** button for punches and kicks.
- Melee tools: cricket bat, lathi and hockey stick.
- Health drains gradually when you take hits. Eat, drink chai or visit a hospital to heal.
- Player-vs-player hits when you play online.

### Super powers and the Raksha Force
Find glowing power orbs around the city and unlock:
- **Jaal web-slinger:** swing between buildings on webs.
- **Surya flight & laser eyes:** fly over the city and fire laser beams.
- **Vayu super speed:** run faster than traffic.

With powers come missions:
- **Rescues:** stop thieves and thugs and save citizens, who clap and cheer for you.
- **Raksha Force calls:** team missions to stop bank robberies, stop hijacks, and help the police.

### The sea
- Coastal cities have sharks, whales and a giant megalodon in the water.
- A huge ocean liner you can board and explore.

### Online play
- **Open online:** everyone who opens the game joins the same India world automatically. No invites or sign-up needed, and up to 16 players can be in a room.
- **Private rooms:** share a room code to play only with friends.
- **Voice chat:** tap the mic to talk with players near you.

### Made for phones
- Touch controls with a joystick and buttons.
- **Settings for button size and see-through level**, so controls never block the view.
- Graphics quality levels (Low / Medium / High / Ultra). Quality drops automatically if the phone slows down.
- **Get unstuck** button, in case you ever end up inside a wall.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left joystick | Move |
| Drag on the right side | Look around (including up at the sky and rooftops) |
| RUN | Sprint |
| HIT | Punch, kick, or swing your tool |
| Action button | Enter doors, vehicles, temples and shops |
| Power buttons | Use super powers once unlocked |
| Map | Open the map and Find places |
| Online | Join the open world or a private room |
| 🎤 | Voice chat |
| ⚙️ | Settings: graphics, button size, see-through |

---

## Built with

- **JavaScript (ES modules)**, HTML and CSS in a single self-contained page with no build step.
- **three.js** for 3D rendering, with post-processing (ambient occlusion, bloom, colour grading and motion trails).
- **WebRTC**, with peer-to-peer rooms through Trystero over Nostr relays, for online play and voice chat.
- **Web Audio** for sound and sirens.
- **OpenStreetMap** data for real streets and buildings.
- Hosted on **GitHub Pages**.
